# Faceless for Claude

Work with the videos in your [Faceless.so](https://faceless.so) account from Claude: find them, render finished videos to MP4, write per-platform titles and captions, publish now or schedule posts to YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, X, Facebook, LinkedIn and Threads, cancel scheduled posts, and see your posting calendar and analytics.

## What it includes

- The `faceless` skill, which teaches Claude the find, render, write, schedule and review workflow.
- The Faceless connector (remote MCP server at https://faceless.so/api/v1/mcp/claude).

## Authenticate

The first time a Faceless tool runs, Claude opens a Faceless sign-in. Sign in, pick the team to connect and approve the permissions. There is no key to configure, and you can revoke access any time from your team settings at https://faceless.so/team.

## Privacy

The plugin talks only to faceless.so through the connector above and stores nothing on your machine. Faceless's privacy policy, covering what it stores, for how long and who it shares data with, is at https://faceless.so/privacy. Support: support@faceless.so.
